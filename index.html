Reading Comprehension

My Daily Routine:

Every morning, I wake up at 6:30 AM. I make my bed, brush my teeth, and wash my face. Then, I eat a healthy breakfast with my family. I usually drink coffee and eat oatmeal with fruit. After breakfast, I take the bus to work. I work from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. In the evening, I come home, cook dinner, and watch TV to relax. I go to bed at 10:00 PM.

Read each statement and select TRUE, FALSE, or NOT GIVEN.

1. I wake up at 6:30 AM every morning.

2. I drink tea with breakfast.

3. I drive my car to work.

4. I work for eight hours.

5. I like to read books before sleeping.

6. I go to bed at 10:00 PM.
