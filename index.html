Reading Comprehension

The History of Chocolate:
Chocolate has a fascinating history that began thousands of years ago. It originally started as a bitter drink in ancient Mesoamerica, where the Olmec and Maya civilizations used cacao beans to make a special, frothy beverage. Later, the Aztecs valued the beans so much that they used them as money. When Spanish explorers brought chocolate to Europe in the sixteenth century, it was a luxury only the rich could enjoy. Over time, people began to add sugar and milk to the recipe, creating the sweet treat we know today.

Read each statement and select TRUE, FALSE, or NOT GIVEN.

1. Ancient Mesoamericans drank chocolate as a sweet beverage.
Explanation: The text mentions that it originally started as a "bitter drink," not a sweet one.
2. Cacao beans were used for currency by the Aztec civilization.
Explanation: The passage explicitly states that the Aztecs "used them as money".
3. Chocolate was easily available to all social classes in sixteenth-century Europe.
Explanation: The text explains that chocolate was "a luxury only the rich could enjoy".
4. Milk was added to chocolate recipes during the Mesoamerican period.
Explanation: The text mentions adding milk "Over time" after it was brought to Europe, but doesn't specify if Mesoamericans added it.
5. European explorers discovered cacao beans in Spain.
Explanation: The explorers brought chocolate *to* Europe from Mesoamerica; they did not find cacao beans in Spain.
6. The Maya civilization was the first to use cacao beans.
Explanation: The text mentions the Olmec and Maya civilizations used cacao beans, but it does not state who began using them first.
